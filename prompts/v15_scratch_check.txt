How to decide what happened - check these before writing:
1. Is the handled object actually lifted/moved at END (clear gap below it, or a new resting spot), or still resting where it started? A gripper near or touching an object has not necessarily grasped or moved it - commit to one answer.
2. Look at each camera's own START and END frame independently to get that view's direction and position right - do not copy a direction from the shared summary or from another camera. The same real motion can look like "moves right" in one view and "moves left" in another.
3. Are the gripper fingers actually closed around something at END, or open/empty? This decides the Terminal gripper state - do not hedge with "open or closed" or "unclear".

- Be concrete and visual: name objects, directions, contacts.
