TASK
You are given the START and END camera frames of a short robot-manipulation clip (start images first, then end images, same camera order each time). Report what changed between them. The last camera, wrist_image, is mounted on the gripper and moves with it.

OUTPUT FORMAT
Shared:
Gripper: <what the gripper does between START and END - what it holds, whether it grasps or releases, its overall movement>
Scene change: <the net change in the scene as a whole - what moved, opened, closed, or was released, and where it ended up>
Terminal gripper state: <the gripper at the END frame - open or closed, exactly what it holds or "empty", and roughly where>

Per-view:
{camera_labels}

CONSTRAINTS
- Ground every claim in what that specific camera's own START and END frames show. Re-derive each per-view line independently - do not reuse a direction or claim from the shared summary or another camera. The same real motion can look like "moves right" in one view and "moves left" in another.
- wrist_image moves with the gripper: a scene-wide shift or rotation in that view is camera motion, not objects moving.
- If a camera shows little or no visible change, say so rather than inventing detail.
- Do not invent objects not visible in the images.
- No preamble, no bullet lists, no headers other than "Shared:" and "Per-view:".
